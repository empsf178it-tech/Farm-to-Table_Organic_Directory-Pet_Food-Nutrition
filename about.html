Pioneering Honest Pet Care

Our Story & Ethical Mission

Bridging the gap between conscious pet guardians and verified regenerative farms to bring unadulterated nourishment into every pet's bowl.

Founded on Transparency

Reclaiming Real Food for Companion Animals

PawsOrganic was created out of frustration with misleading pet food labels, mystery meat renders, and chemical preservatives disguised as healthy nutrition. We asked a simple question: why shouldn't our dogs and cats enjoy the same farm-fresh transparency we demand for our own plates?

Today, our nationwide directory connects pet parents with local regenerative farms, small-batch artisanal kitchens, and holistic animal dieticians dedicated to nutrient bioavailability and environmental harmony.

Our Ingredient Sourcing Standards

We believe animals thrive when food stays close to nature's biological rhythms.

🌿

Certified Organic Soils

Heirloom squash, carrots, and kelp raised in living soil devoid of synthetic chemical weedkillers or glyphosate.

🐓

Pasture-Raised Welfare

Chickens, cattle, and turkeys roam free under open skies with cruelty-free handling, zero hormone additives, and natural diets.

🐟

Sustainable Ocean Harvest

Our fish suppliers employ selective hook-and-line or certified sustainable netting to protect delicate marine ecosystems.

🔬

Third-Party Laboratory Testing

Batch audits check for heavy metal purity, microbial safety, and genuine nutrient density before any product is listed.

Eco Commitments

Closing the Loop on Plastic & Waste

Pet nutrition shouldn't cost the Earth. Over 80% of our partnered suppliers package meals in backyard-compostable pouches, recyclable tins, or insulated chilled shippers crafted from reclaimed ocean plastics.

  • ✓ 100% Carbon-Neutral regional courier partnerships
  • ✓ Zero landfill initiatives across all verified commercial kitchens
  • ✓ Support for smallholder family farming co-operatives
